LUQMA PROJECT PLAN
==================

Project name: Luqma - لقمة
Project type: WhatsApp-style restaurant ordering MVP
Current scope: One restaurant, demo only, fake data, no real integrations
Target audience: Restaurant owners, investors, and customers

1. PROJECT IDEA
---------------

Luqma is a commission-free restaurant ordering experience inspired by WhatsApp.
Instead of relying on phone calls or third-party delivery platforms, the customer
opens a conversation with the restaurant, browses a visual menu, customizes items,
builds a cart, and submits an order through a familiar chat interface.

The current product is an investor demo. It should look and behave like a real
WhatsApp ordering conversation, but it does not need real WhatsApp, payment,
database, authentication, or restaurant integrations yet.

2. PROBLEM AND OPPORTUNITY
--------------------------

Restaurants commonly face two expensive ordering options:

1. Phone ordering requires staff to answer calls, repeat the menu, record orders,
   and handle mistakes manually.
2. Third-party delivery platforms can charge large commissions and control the
   customer relationship and order data.

Luqma aims to provide a direct-to-customer ordering channel owned by the restaurant,
with a predictable cost and a faster ordering experience.

3. MVP OBJECTIVES
-----------------

The MVP must demonstrate these points to investors:

- Ordering feels like a natural WhatsApp conversation.
- Customers can start by typing any message, including a single character.
- The bot responds with a bilingual language-selection message.
- Arabic and English are supported.
- Customers can browse a visual product catalog.
- Customers can select quantities and review a cart.
- Customers can customize products using realistic options.
- Each customized item keeps its own options in the cart.
- The final order collects the customer name, mobile number, and payment method.
- Cash completes the demo order.
- Apple Pay and Mada show a demo payment step that can later connect to an API.

4. CUSTOMER USER FLOW
---------------------

The intended conversation is:

1. The customer opens the Luqma chat.
2. The customer types any message and presses Send.
3. The message appears as an outgoing WhatsApp-style bubble.
4. The bot replies first in Arabic and then in English:
   - Arabic welcome message
   - Arabic question asking which language the customer prefers
   - English welcome message
   - English language question
5. The customer chooses Arabic or English using buttons.
6. The selected language appears as an outgoing message.
7. The bot says “Here is the menu” in the selected language.
8. The bot displays an All Products catalog card with an image, item count,
   description, and a View Items button.
9. The customer presses View Items.
10. The product list opens with images, product names, prices, and quantity controls.
11. The customer can press plus or minus to change quantities.
12. The customer can open the cart.
13. The cart displays every selected item, its quantity, customizations, line price,
    and the estimated total.
14. The customer sends the cart.
15. The bot asks for:
    - Name
    - Mobile number
    - Payment method
16. The payment options are:
    - Cash at the restaurant
    - Apple Pay
    - Mada
17. Cash displays a successful order confirmation.
18. Apple Pay and Mada display a demo payment page. In a future phase, these buttons
    should redirect to a real payment gateway API.

6. BURGER CUSTOMIZATION
-----------------------

When the customer selects a burger, show a product customization screen or card.

Cheese options:

- No cheese — 0 SAR
- Regular cheese — 0 SAR
- Extra cheese — 2 SAR

Spice options:

- Mild
- Regular
- Max

Add-ons:

- Extra beef patty — 5.5 SAR

Ingredient removal options:

- No tomato
- No onion
- No sauce
- No specific ingredient

The customer should also have a free-text notes field for requests such as:

- Sauce on the side
- No pickles
- Cut in half

Only paid options should increase the item price. Removing an ingredient or choosing
the regular/default option must not add a charge.

7. PIZZA CUSTOMIZATION
----------------------

When the customer selects pizza, show flavor and size options.

Pizza flavors:

- Pepperoni
- Margherita
- Chicken Ranch
- Four Cheese

Pizza sizes and prices:

- Small — 16 SAR
- Medium — 19 SAR
- Large — 23 SAR

The pizza price displayed in the menu should show the range 16–23 SAR. After the
customer selects a size, the selected size price must become the item price.

Pizza options:

- Spice level: Mild, Regular, Max
- Add-ons can be selected with clear prices when applicable

Suggested pizza add-ons:

- Mozzarella — 3 SAR
- Sausage — 4 SAR
- Chicken — 5 SAR
- Olives — 2 SAR

Do not charge for spice level or pizza flavor. Charge only for paid add-ons.

8. SOFT DRINK CUSTOMIZATION
--------------------------

When the customer presses Soft Drinks, show drink choices instead of treating all
soft drinks as one generic item.

Available choices:

- Cola — 3 SAR
- Pepsi — 3 SAR
- Sprite — 3 SAR
- Mountain Dew — 3 SAR
- Kenza — 2.5 SAR

Ice options should not add a fee:

- Regular ice
- No ice
- Extra ice

Flavor or drink selection must not add an unexpected fee. The price must be visible
before adding the item to the cart.

9. CART AND EDITING RULES
-------------------------

The cart must support:

- Increasing quantity
- Decreasing quantity
- Removing an item when quantity reaches zero
- Showing the price for each line item
- Showing the complete customization details
- Showing the total price
- Adding more products
- Editing an existing product without resetting its configuration

Every customized item must remain independent.

Example:

- Burger 1: no cheese, no tomato, mild spice
- Burger 2: extra cheese, no onion, max spice

Editing Burger 1 must not change Burger 2.

The cart must calculate prices correctly:

item price + paid add-ons, multiplied by quantity

Free selections must never increase the total.

10. CUSTOMER DETAILS AND PAYMENT
--------------------------------

At the end of the flow, ask only for the information needed for the demo:

- Name
- Mobile number
- Payment method

Payment method should use a clear selection control with three choices:

- Cash at restaurant
- Apple Pay
- Mada

Demo behavior:

- Cash: complete the order and show a success message.
- Apple Pay: show a demo payment page and explain that it will connect to a payment
  API later.
- Mada: show a demo payment page and explain that it will connect to a payment API
  later.

The MVP must not collect or store real card numbers, CVV, or expiry dates because
there is no real payment integration yet.

11. VISUAL DESIGN DIRECTION
---------------------------

The customer experience should look like a real WhatsApp chat inside an iPhone-like
Flutter-inspired device frame.

Phone frame:

- Tall rounded iPhone proportions
- Dark realistic outer frame
- Dynamic Island-style top cutout
- Realistic shadow
- Smooth corner radius
- Stable header, chat area, and composer layout

Header:

- iPhone-style status bar
- Real-time device time when available
- Battery indicator
- Signal and Wi-Fi indicators matching the header color
- Green WhatsApp-inspired header
- Circular restaurant avatar
- “Luqma - لقمة” title
- Online status
- Back button
- Restart conversation control

Chat area:

- WhatsApp wallpaper background
- Incoming white bubbles
- Outgoing green bubbles
- Message timestamps
- Double-check indicators on outgoing messages
- Catalog cards inside the conversation
- Clear button feedback

Landing page:

- Keep a clean investor-demo layout around the phone.
- Improve the hero typography, spacing, and responsive behavior.
- Keep the phone as the main visual focus.
- Make the complete webpage consistent with the phone UI.

12. TECHNOLOGY DIRECTION
------------------------

For the future full product, the recommended architecture is:

- Frontend: Next.js
- Backend: FastAPI with Python
- Database: PostgreSQL or another production database
- Messaging: Official WhatsApp Cloud API
- Payments: Saudi payment gateway supporting Mada and Apple Pay
- Restaurant dashboard: Web dashboard for incoming orders and order status

For the current MVP, a single HTML, CSS, and JavaScript demo is acceptable. The MVP
uses local fake data and does not need a backend.

13. CURRENT LIMITATIONS AND KNOWN PROBLEMS
------------------------------------------

1. The UI/UX still needs significant improvement. It currently looks like an MVP
   prototype and is not yet at a polished production or investor-ready level.

2. The Send button has had a serious bug where pressing it reloads or resets the
   page instead of continuing the conversation. This must be tested and fixed
   permanently.

3. The phone frame is not completely convincing or realistic. The proportions,
   Dynamic Island, status bar, battery, icons, spacing, and bottom composer still
   need refinement.

4. The complete webpage needs UI/UX improvements, including typography, spacing,
   responsive layout, visual hierarchy, accessibility, and consistency between the
   landing page and the phone interface.

5. The demo currently has no real integrations. WhatsApp, payments, database,
   authentication, notifications, and restaurant operations are simulated.

6. Product customization, cart editing, and price calculation need full end-to-end
   testing for all combinations.

14. PHASE 2
----------

Phase 2 can include:

- Official WhatsApp Cloud API integration
- Real WhatsApp interactive messages and product catalogs
- Backend order service
- Restaurant owner dashboard
- Real-time order notifications
- Menu management
- Product option management
- Customer order history
- Payment gateway integration for Mada and Apple Pay
- Order status updates
- Authentication and restaurant accounts
- Analytics and sales reporting

15. IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITY
---------------------------

Before adding real integrations:

1. Stabilize the Send button and conversation state.
2. Test Arabic and English flows.
3. Test every product and every quantity action.
4. Test burger, pizza, and drink customizations.
5. Verify all paid add-ons update the total.
6. Verify free options do not update the total.
7. Verify editing preserves existing customizations.
8. Improve the phone frame and full-page UI/UX.
9. Prepare a smooth investor demo flow.
10. Only then begin Phase 2 integrations.
